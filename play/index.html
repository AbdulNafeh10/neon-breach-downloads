Master your suit

01 / BUILD MOMENTUM

Stay alive. Get rewarded.

2 kills: +25 armor. 3 kills: 12s haste. 5 kills: 12s surge. Dying resets your streak and clears active powers.

02 / PRESS E

Overdrive on demand.

Your suit charges passively and from combat. At 100%, E gives 6s haste and refreshes dash. Haste means +22% move speed and a 1.5s dash cooldown.

03 / CONTEST THE DROP

Power has a location.

Two glowing pedestals rotate armor, haste, and surge. First drops arrive at 8s; collected drops return in 30s. Purple: +40 armor (60 max). Mint: 12s haste. Amber: 10s of +25% damage.

04 / CHANGE THE ANGLE

Take flight.

Blue jump pads launch you into aerial routes. Green crosses heal 35 HP. Your map shows equipment and briefly reveals a rival when they shoot.

Five guns: 1 rifle, 2 shotgun, 3 precision, 4 SMG, 5 pistol. Choose a starting weapon in Armory. R reloads; right mouse aims. Q dashes; C slides; Shift sprints. Duel, Deathmatch, Team Deathmatch, King of the Hill, Instagib and Shotgun Skirmish are available. The host controls player limits, score targets, time limits, respawns, weapon restrictions and hill rotation in the room lobby. Instagib locks precision rifles and eliminates in one hit; Shotgun Skirmish locks shotguns. Player limits are ceilings: fewer players and uneven teams can start. Everyone present must ready up. Join and change teams between rounds. After each online round, vote for the next map, Continue to the same room, and ready up again. Team Deathmatch pings: G location, Shift+G danger, Alt+G regroup; H acknowledge and X cancel. Spawn protection defaults to 1.5 seconds and blocks damage and firing; the host can adjust it. Save reusable match presets in Match Setup. Signed-in career progress is stored in your account. Share stats and achievements only if you enable profile sharing.
